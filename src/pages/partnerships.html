02 · How the model is shaped

Built around your sites, not a price list

There is no standard partnership document, because a hotel group, a station operator and a festival want different things from the same hardware. We shape it around three questions.

  1. 01Footfall. How many people pass through, and how long they stay. This sets how many machines a site takes and which model fits.
  2. 02Environment. Indoors on a counter, free standing in a lobby, or outdoors on a platform in the rain. We have a machine for each.
  3. 03What you want out of it. Guest experience, income from rentals, screen advertising, or coverage across a region. The commercial terms follow from that.
