02 · How it works

Three steps, and only the first one is yours

You make the introduction. We survey the site, agree the hosting terms, install the station and support it for as long as it is there.

  1. 01Introduce the venue. Send us the site and the person who can say yes. We log it to you straight away, and we tell you if we are already in conversation with them.
  2. 02We take it from there. Site visit, one page hosting agreement, install within two weeks. Nothing is drilled or wired in, and the venue pays nothing.
  3. 03You get paid. Commission starts once the station is live and taking rentals, and continues while it is there.

04 · What makes a good introduction

Four things we look for

  1. 01People who stay a while. Gyms, hotels, cafés, bars, cinemas, co-working floors, waiting rooms and transport hubs all work.

  3. 03Half a square metre of floor near where people wait, and a mains socket within reach.
  4. 04Someone who can sign. One page, one signature, and no legal department needed.

05 · FAQs

Questions referrers ask

How much commission do you pay?

It depends on the venue type and the size of the site, and we agree it with you before you introduce anyone. We don't publish a single rate, because a café and a transport hub are not the same job.

When do I get paid?

Once the station is installed and taking rentals. Nothing is paid on an introduction that never reaches a live site, which is why we are honest with you early about whether a venue will clear our usage target.

What if you are already talking to my venue?

We tell you when you submit it. Introductions are logged to whoever sends them first, and we will say plainly if a site is already in conversation with us.

Do I have to sell anything?

No. Pass us the site and the right contact, and we handle the call, the site visit, the agreement and the install. You are welcome to stay in the conversation if you would rather.

Is there any cost or exclusivity?

Neither. Joining is free, there is no minimum number of introductions, and you are not tied to us.
