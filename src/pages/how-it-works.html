How it works

Simple for guests.
Nothing for you.

A guest can rent a bank in about a minute without an app. A venue goes from first call to a working station in about two weeks, and never has to touch it after that.

01 · For your guests

Four steps, about a minute

Step 01

Scan the code

The QR code is on the front of the machine. The phone camera opens it, and that's the whole setup.

Step 02

Pay on one link

One secure payment page. Apple Pay, Google Pay, Mastercard or a debit card, in pounds or euros.

Step 03

Choose the time

One hour minimum at £3. If they keep it longer, the card is billed automatically for each extra hour.

Step 04

The bank pops out

A timer starts on their phone, with an optional reminder. They can return it to any station on the network to stop the clock.

Tariff

One hour minimum, billed by the hour after that. No deposit and no membership.

£3per hour · ≈ €3.50
02 · For your venue

From first call to switched on

There's one page to sign and nothing to pay, and every rental after that earns you money. This is what happens between the first call and your first share of the income.

Book a free consultation →
  1. 01Free consultation call. We talk about your venue, your footfall and where people tend to wait. If a station makes sense, we suggest a machine.
  2. 02Site visit. We look at the room with you and mark the spot. We need half a square metre and a mains socket within reach.
  3. 03Hosting agreement. One page and one signature. No purchase, no lease and no service contract.
  4. 04Install. Within two weeks of signing. It takes a morning, and nothing is drilled or wired into your building.
  5. 05Two-week trial. The station runs as normal and we collect the usage data.
  6. 06Review and revenue share. We go through the numbers with you and agree the revenue share split for your location. From then on, you earn a share of every rental.
03 · What we handle

Your staff are never the support line

Once the station is in, looking after it is our job. Guests who need help contact us, not your front desk.

Monitored 24/7

Every station reports to us remotely, so we can see when banks run low or a slot needs attention.

Restocked and repaired

Scheduled visits keep the station full and working. Every bank is tested when it comes back.

Insured by us

Theft, damage and liability are ours, including banks that leave the building. Every station is installed with £5m public and products liability cover.
