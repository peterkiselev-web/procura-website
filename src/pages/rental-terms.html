How the time is counted

The clock starts when the machine releases the power bank and stops when the machine records it back. Any started hour counts as a full hour. If you return it after 40 minutes you pay only the first hour. If you return it after 2 hours and 10 minutes you pay for three hours.

Your saved card

When you pay, your card or wallet is saved with our payment provider, Stripe, so we can take the charges described on this page without you being there. We never see or store your full card number. We take any extra rent once, when you return the power bank. If it hasn't come back by the cut-off, we take the rent still owed and the non-return fee once, then close the rental. We don't charge every hour, and nothing more is charged after that, even if the power bank is returned later. Stripe emails you a receipt for each charge.

If something goes wrong

  • If you pay and the machine can't release a power bank, we refund your payment in full automatically. It can take 5 to 10 working days to appear on your statement.
  • If a later charge is declined, we try a small number of times and then ask you to get in touch. We don't keep trying indefinitely.
  • If you think you have been charged wrongly, email us and we will look into it.

Looking after the power bank

Please return the power bank to a Procura machine in the condition you received it. Use it for charging phones and similar devices only.
