Hosting for hotels

Power bank rental
for hotels

Guests need their phones for tickets, taxis, room keys and the way home. A Procura station keeps them charged, costs you nothing, and pays you a share of every rental.

Book a free consultation →See the machines

Yes, a hotel can host a power bank rental station for free. Procura installs and runs it, and pays you a share of every rental.

A cable behind the desk is not a solution

When a guest's phone dies in your building, the best anyone can offer is a cable behind the front desk and a socket someone has to sit next to. A bank they can take with them solves the problem without anyone guarding a cable.

In June 2026 Vodafone UK found that 74% of people are wary of going too far from a plug socket because of battery worries, and 46% say their phone nearly dies at least once a week. Source: Vodafone UK, survey of 2,000 UK adults by Walr, June 2026.

Which machine fits a hotel

For the lobby, the Procura Floor 20 is a slim standing kiosk with twenty banks under a full-height portrait screen. For the bar or restaurant, the Procura Counter holds twelve banks in a cabinet about the size of a coffee machine.

See all the machines. We look at the building with you on the site visit and mark the exact spots.

What it costs you, and how you earn

  • It costs £0 to host, for as long as you keep it. No purchase, no lease, no service contract and no invoices.
  • You take a share of every rental. The split varies by location, and we agree it with you after a consultation call and a two-week trial.
  • The screen is yours. Run your own offers, or sell the space to sponsors and keep that money as well.
  • Your branding goes on the machine. Colours and logo are agreed before it is built.
  • The lobby screen can carry your own offers, or sponsors and suppliers.

What your team has to do

Nothing. Guests scan the QR code, pay on their phone and take a bank, with no app to download. If anything goes wrong they deal with us, not your team. We monitor the station remotely, restock and repair it, and cover theft, damage and liability, with £5m public and products liability cover on every installation.

How to get started

  1. A free consultation call about your venue and your footfall.
  2. A site visit, where we mark the spot together.
  3. A one-page hosting agreement, with no purchase, lease or service contract.
  4. Installation, which takes a morning with nothing drilled or wired in. Then a two-week trial, and a review of the numbers.

We order machines one at a time, in the order hosting agreements are signed, so we give you a realistic window when you join rather than a date we might miss.

Quick answers

Before you ask

Where does it go in a hotel?

Most hotels put a Floor 20 kiosk in the lobby and a Counter unit at the bar or restaurant. We look at the building with you on the site visit and mark the spots.

Does the hotel have to handle guest payments?

No. Guests pay on their own phone, on one secure payment page, and your team is never involved.

What about banks that leave the building?

Their card carries on being billed by the hour, and theft, damage and liability are ours, including banks that leave the building.
