Rental terms

Renting a
power bank

Who you are renting from

Power banks at Procura stations are rented by JUKIE Experiences Ltd, which trades as Procura (a private limited company registered in England and Wales, company number 16203343, registered office 21 Royal Avenue, London, SW3 4QE). Questions: peter@procuracharge.com.

What it costs

  • You pay £3.00 when you rent. That covers the first hour.
  • After that, each further hour or part of an hour is £3.00. Rent for one rental is never more than £20.00.
  • If you don't return the power bank within 12 hours, we also charge a non-return fee of £20.00.
  • The most we can charge for one rental is £40.00 (£20.00 rent plus the £20.00 fee).

All prices are in pounds sterling.

How the time is counted

The clock starts when the machine releases the power bank and stops when the machine records it back. Any started hour counts as a full hour. If you return it after 40 minutes you pay only the first hour. If you return it after 2 hours and 10 minutes you pay for three hours.

Your saved card

When you pay, your card or wallet is saved with our payment provider, Stripe, so we can take the charges described on this page without you being there. We never see or store your full card number. By paying you agree to this wording, which is also shown next to the pay button and kept with your rental:

I agree to the rental terms. Procura Charge may charge this card £3.00 now, and later, without me present, £3.00 for each further started hour (rent is never more than £20.00). If I have not returned the power bank within 12 hours, I will also be charged a £20.00 non-return fee, so the most I can be charged is £40.00.

We take any extra rent once, when you return the power bank. If it hasn't come back by the cut-off, we take the rent still owed and the non-return fee once, then close the rental. We don't charge every hour, and nothing more is charged after that, even if the power bank is returned later. Stripe emails you a receipt for each charge.

If something goes wrong

  • If you pay and the machine can't release a power bank, we refund your payment in full automatically. It can take 5 to 10 working days to appear on your statement.
  • If a later charge is declined, we try a small number of times and then ask you to get in touch. We don't keep trying indefinitely.
  • If you think you have been charged wrongly, email us and we will look into it.

Looking after the power bank

Please return the power bank to a Procura machine in the condition you received it. Use it for charging phones and similar devices only.

Your details

How we handle your email address and payment information is explained in our privacy notice.
