01 · What it costs you

£0 to host, for as long as you keep it

There's nothing to buy, lease or service, and we never send you an invoice. We own the hardware and carry the risk, and you take a share of the rental income. All we need is half a square metre of floor near where people wait, and a mains socket.

02 · The power bank

Three cables in every bank

Lightning, USB-C and micro-USB are built into each 10,000 mAh bank, so an iPhone and an Android can share one. That's two full charges of an iPhone 17, and roughly 80% in 30 minutes on a 22.5W fast charge.

03 · Your brand

Your logo on the front, not ours

We control our own manufacturing, so colour, finish, panel graphics and logo are agreed before the unit is built. The 21-inch screen runs your promotions, classes or menu, and you can sell it to your own sponsors and keep the money.

07 · How it works

Four steps, about a minute, no app

Nothing to download, no deposit, and no reason for anyone to ask your staff for help.

Step 01

Scan the code

The QR code is on the front of the machine. The phone camera opens it, and that's the whole setup.

Step 02

Pay on one link

A single secure payment page. Apple Pay, Google Pay, Mastercard or a debit card, in pounds or euros.

Step 03

Choose the time

One hour minimum at £3. If they keep it longer, the card is billed automatically for each extra hour.

Step 04

The bank pops out

A timer starts on their phone, with an optional reminder. They return it to any station on the network to stop the clock.

Tariff

One hour minimum, billed by the hour after that. No deposit and no membership.

10 · FAQs

Questions venues ask us

Can't see yours? Ask us directly and our team will get back to you.

How much does one of your machines cost?

Nothing. Our machines are free, and they cost you £0 to host. That's the fun part.

All we need from you is a signed hosting agreement. In return your customers are more engaged and stay longer, because nobody has to leave when their phone runs out.

We also offer revenue share agreements, with splits that depend on your type of business. We share those figures after a consultation call.

How do I make money from a station?

Three ways. You take a share of the income from every rental at your station. Your customers stay longer and spend more, because a flat phone no longer ends their visit. And you can sell the screen on the station to sponsors or use it for your own promotions.

How is the revenue share split decided?

It varies from location to location. Your type of venue, your footfall and where the station can go all play a part. We go through it with you on a consultation call and set the split for your site.

Who pays for installation, repairs and insurance?

We do. We deliver, position and switch the station on in a morning, and nothing gets drilled or wired in. We monitor every station remotely and restock and repair it on a schedule.

Theft, damage and liability are ours, including banks that leave the building. Every station is installed with £5m public and products liability cover.

What do my staff have to do?

Nothing. Guests scan the QR code, pay on their phone and take a bank. If anything goes wrong they deal with us, not your front desk.

How much space does it need?

Half a square metre of floor and a mains socket within reach. The counter station can also sit on a shelf or bar top. We mark the exact spot with you on the site visit.

What does it cost my guests?

£3 an hour, with a one hour minimum and billing by the hour after that. There's no deposit and no membership, and they can return the bank to any Procura station on the network.

What if someone doesn't bring a bank back?

Their card carries on being billed by the hour, and any loss is ours to deal with. It never becomes your problem.

Can we try it before we commit?

Yes. The standard trial is two weeks. Afterwards we go through the usage data with you and agree the revenue share split for your location.

How quickly can you install?

The hosting agreement is one page with one signature. Once it's signed, your station goes in within two weeks.

Do you have outdoor machines?

Yes. The Tower 40 is waterproof and weatherproof, and it is built for train stations and large commercial outdoor sites. We survey the site first to choose the best spot for footfall and power.

Can the machine match our branding?

Yes. Your colours go across the body and panel and your logo goes on the front. The screen runs your promotions, classes or menu, and you can sell screen time to your own sponsors and keep the revenue.

Which phones does it charge?

Every bank has Lightning, USB-C and micro-USB cables built in, so it charges iPhones and Android phones, old and new. Each 10,000 mAh bank holds two full charges of an iPhone 17.

Are the power banks safe?

The cells are certified, with overcharge and temperature protection. We test every bank when it comes back and take any unit that drops below capacity out of the network.

Are you already running in the UK?

We're opening in London now. Procura already runs stations in more than 30 venues, and our first UK hosts join as founding partners.
