01 · The idea

We started Procura around one idea. Busy venues want happier guests and better ratings, and most ways of getting there cost money and effort.

Hosting our machine costs neither. You give us the space, we do the rest, and your guests get something they actually needed.

We started in 2025 and now run stations in more than 30 venues, on gym floors and in hotel lobbies, cafés, bars and cinemas. London is next.

02 · Why we make our own

From cells to cabinet

We control our own manufacturing chain in China. It lets us build a station in a venue's own colours with its logo on the front, and it means we choose the cells that go into every bank.

Those cells are certified, with overcharge and temperature protection. Every bank is tested when it comes back, and anything that drops below capacity comes out of the network.

03 · London

Our first UK hosts are founding partners

We're choosing our first London venues now. Founding partners get the same deal as everyone who follows: £0 to host, installation once your machine arrives, and a revenue share on every rental once the two-week trial is done.
