02 · Who it suits

People with sites and the will to run them

The strongest territory partners tend to have two things: access to venues in their region, and the appetite to be out in those venues regularly.

  1. 01Entrepreneurs and operators. You want a network you own in a city you know.
  2. 02Hospitality businesses. You already run venues, and you can put stations into your own sites while selling into others.
  3. 03Regional infrastructure partners. You service machines, screens or vending already, and this fits the rounds you drive anyway.
  4. 04Groups with site access. Property, events or leisure portfolios where the venues are already yours to approach.
03 · How territories work

Agreed case by case

We set territories by region, by the scale you can take on and by how you plan to run it. A single city centre and a county are different commitments, so we do not publish one fixed package.

What we will not do is sell you a territory you cannot service. If the region is bigger than the team you have, we would rather start you smaller and extend it once the first stations are working.

04 · The process

From first call to first station

  1. 01A conversation. Your region, your experience and the venues you can already reach.
  2. 02Scope the territory. We look at the footfall in that area and agree what you can realistically cover in year one.
  3. 03Terms in writing. Hardware, platform, support and commercials, all set out before you commit to anything.
  4. 04Launch. We help you place and install the first stations, then you run the network with our support behind you.
