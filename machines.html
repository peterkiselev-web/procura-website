The machines

Three sizes.
One way to rent.

Every Procura machine works the same way for a guest: scan, pay, take a bank. Every one is free to host and pays you a share of its rentals. What changes is how many banks it holds and where it can go.

01 · Indoor · counter or shelf

Procura Counter

Our standard station and the one most venues start with. Twelve banks sit in a cabinet about the size of a coffee machine, under a 21-inch screen. When the room gets busy, a second cabinet stacks on top to make 24.

Drag the model to turn it.

Slots12 per unit, stackable to 24
CablesLightning, USB-C and micro-USB in every bank
Screen21″, yours to programme outside peak hours
Space0.5 m² of floor, or a shelf or bar top, plus a mains socket
ServiceMonitored remotely 24/7, restocked and serviced by our team
Best forCafés, bars, restaurants, gym floors, co-working spaces
02 · Indoor · floor standing

Procura Floor 20

A slim standing kiosk for places where people queue, wait or check in. Twenty banks sit in two columns under a full-height portrait screen, which is big enough to carry proper advertising.

Slots20
CablesLightning, USB-C and micro-USB in every bank
ScreenFull-height portrait advertising screen
SpaceA small patch of floor and a mains socket, confirmed on the site visit
Best forHotel lobbies, gym receptions, shopping centres, hospitals, universities
03 · Outdoor · waterproof and weatherproof

Procura Tower 40

Our largest machine, built for train stations and large commercial outdoor sites where thousands of people pass every day. It is waterproof and made to stand up to British weather, so it can go on a platform, a forecourt or an open-air concourse. Forty banks sit in four columns under a large screen you can sell to advertisers.

More people passing means more rentals, and more rentals means a bigger revenue share for the site.

Slots40
CablesLightning, USB-C and micro-USB in every bank
ScreenLarge portrait advertising screen
PlacementIndoors or fully outdoors
BuildWaterproof and weatherproof, made for rain, cold and summer heat
Best forTrain stations, bus and coach stations, airports, stadiums, retail parks and outdoor commercial sites

06 · Your brand, not ours

Built to match your interior

Procura runs its own manufacturing chain in China, from cells to cabinet. That means a station isn't a fixed product we drop into your space. Colour, finish, panel graphics and logo are all agreed before the unit is built.
