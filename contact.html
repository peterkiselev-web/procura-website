Quick answers

Before you ask

How much does one of your machines cost?

Nothing. Our machines are free, and they cost you £0 to host. That's the fun part.

All we need from you is a signed hosting agreement. In return your customers are more engaged and stay longer, because nobody has to leave when their phone runs out.

We also offer revenue share agreements, with splits that depend on your type of business. We share those figures after a consultation call.

How long is the consultation call?

Usually a short call. We'd rather hear about your venue than give a presentation, and there's no obligation afterwards.
